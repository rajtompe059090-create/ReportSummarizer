Privacy Policy for DailyKit

Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to DailyKit: Everyday tools, made easy ("DailyKit", "we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring you have complete transparency into how your data is handled.

1. Local-First Architecture & No Data Collection

DailyKit is built strictly on a local-first architecture. All your calculations (Calculator, EMI, GST, Discount, Percentage, Age, Date Difference, Countdown, BMI, Unit Conversions) and stored items (Notes, Saved Favorites, Recent Tools) are stored and computed entirely on your physical device.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to sign up, log in, or provide any personal details like your name, email, or phone number to use DailyKit.
  • No Cloud Sync: We do not run custom servers or cloud databases that store or read your calculations or notes.
  • Full User Control: You can delete any note, favorite, or history record at any time from within the application, or by clearing application data in your Android system settings.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

DailyKit uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements which support the ongoing development of this application.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data, such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address (for coarse geographic location and fraud prevention)
  • Ad interaction telemetry and performance metrics

For information on how Google manages and protects data in ads, please review Google's Privacy Policy at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Device Permissions

DailyKit does not require or request any dangerous or intrusive device permissions (such as Location, Camera, Contacts, Microphone, or Storage). The only permission requested is standard Internet access (android.permission.INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) which is used solely by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and render banner/interstitial advertisements.

4. Children's Privacy

DailyKit is a general utility app suitable for all audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be reflected here with an updated "Effective Date".
